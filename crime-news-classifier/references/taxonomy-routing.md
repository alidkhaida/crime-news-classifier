# Taxonomy routing

The bundled archive documents are the maintained source material:

- `archive/Crime_News_Classification_Taxonomy.md`: main categories, category boundaries, overlaps, and negative routing.
- `archive/Crime_News_Subcategory_Classification_Guide.md`: approved subcategory names and definitions.
- `archive/rules.md`: final client inclusion/exclusion policy; load only for the policy stage.
- The supplied Google Doc `CRIME CATEGORIES - ROUGH DRAFT`: narrative clarification of the same 19-category recognition layer; it adds no approved subcategory list.

Priority is: current user instruction, then the maintained local taxonomy/subcategory guide, then the narrative Google Doc for interpretation. If sources conflict materially, preserve the record, mark the conflict, and request policy resolution rather than silently choosing.

## Recognition rules

- Identify what allegedly happened, who was involved, the relationship/context, and the case stage.
- Assign every supported substantive offense and supported context label.
- Do not infer a category from a single keyword, job title, location, age appearance, weapon mention, or family relationship.
- A child victim alone is not Child Maltreatment. Add Child Maltreatment when abuse, neglect, abandonment, failure to protect, or dangerous exposure is connected to an adult with responsibility, duty, control, or meaningful access.
- A dangerous unsecured weapon accessible to a child can support Child Maltreatment in addition to a weapon offense when that adult-responsibility nexus is present.
- A death described as accidental is not automatically Homicide. Use Homicide/Death for criminal, suspicious, attempted-killing, fatal-shooting, or unresolved criminal-death facts supported by the record.
- A missing person is not automatically kidnapping; retain missing status separately.
- A workplace or institutional context requires a meaningful access, duty, authority, victim, security, or conduct nexus.
- An offender’s age or juvenile status must be explicit; do not infer it from “young” or a photo.

## New-subcategory rule

When no approved subcategory fits, assign the best supported main category and save an unmapped candidate with parent category, proposed name, evidence phrase, and confidence. Do not invent an official subcategory in the category cell.
